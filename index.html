Edition & Censorship Dossier

From Obelisk Press (Paris, 1939) to Flamingo (London, 1993)

Original Publication (1939): Completed at Villa Seurat in September 1938, Tropic of Capricorn was first published in Paris by Jack Kahane’s Obelisk Press (16 Place Vendôme) in February 1939 with the cover warning: "NOT TO BE IMPORTED INTO GREAT BRITAIN OR U.S.A."

Censorship & Legal Editions (1962–1964): Customs bans kept the novel out of English-speaking bookstores for over two decades until Grove Press published it in the United States in September 1962 and John Calder issued the first British edition in 1964.

Edition Scanned on This Site (1993): The 216-page PDF hosted here reproduces the unabridged Flamingo Modern Classic paperback (HarperCollins, London, 1993, ISBN 0-00-654584-X), featuring an introduction by Robert Nye on PDF page 2, Miller’s opening text on PDF page 3, and the final Villa Seurat dateline on PDF page 216.
