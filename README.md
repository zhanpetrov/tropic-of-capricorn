# TROPIC OF CAPRICORN — Henry Miller | Book Immersion, Audio & Notes Studio

An Apple-inspired literary immersion studio, **Cosmodemonic Wire** showcase, side-by-side **PDF + Margin Notes Working Desk**, 1920s New York psychogeography map, and multi-layered audio companion (Audiobooks + Miles Davis) for Henry Miller's *Tropic of Capricorn* ("On the Ovarian Trolley") and *Tropic of Cancer*.

- **Live Website:** <https://zhanpetrov.github.io/tropic-of-capricorn/>
- **Repository:** <https://github.com/zhanpetrov/tropic-of-capricorn>

## Edition Credits

- **Original Publication:** Obelisk Press, 16 Place Vendôme, Paris, February 1939.
- **Edition Scanned on This Site:** Flamingo Modern Classic (HarperCollins, London, 1993, ISBN `0-00-654584-X`, 216 PDF viewer pages), with an introduction by Robert Nye on PDF page 2, Miller's opening text on PDF page 3, and the final Villa Seurat dateline on PDF page 216.

## Book Immersion Working Desk (Notes, Scratchpad & Marginalia)

Designed for active deep reading so you never have to leave your book or audio stream to write:

- **Top-Bar `✎ Notes` Pill & `⌘J` / `Ctrl+J` Shortcut** — Accessible from every screen with a live saved-notes badge.
- **Home Immersion Notes & Scratchpad (`#homeNotesWorkspace`)** — Positioned right below the Home hero stage with:
  - **Live Auto-Saving Scratchpad** (`capricorn_scratchpad_v1`) synced across Home, the PDF Margin Desk, and the floating Quick Notes sheet.
  - **Book & Page Context Tagging** (`Tropic of Capricorn · PDF p. X`, `Tropic of Cancer`, or `Reflection`) + **Category Tags** (`💡 Insight`, `📖 Passage`, `✍️ Idea / Draft`, `❓ Question`).
  - **Automatic Audio Memory Stamps** — Notes saved while listening to an audiobook or Miles Davis track store clickable badges (`🎧 Capricorn II`, `🎺 Blue in Green`) so one tap restores the exact reading & listening atmosphere.
  - **Search, Pin (`★`), Inline Edit, Markdown (`.MD`) Export, `.TXT` Export & JSON Backup/Import.**
- **Side-by-Side PDF + Margin Notes Split Desk (`#pdfSplitGrid`)** — Inside **II. Full PDF · 216 pages**, your Margin Notes Desk sits right beside the scanned book (`8 cols` PDF + `4 cols` Margin Notes) synced to the active PDF page (`Save to Pg. 29 · ⌘↵`), with `All Notes` vs `This Page` filtering and a `Full PDF ↔` toggle.
- **Floating Frosted-Glass Quick Notes Sheet (`#quickNotesDrawer`)** — Opens right alongside the **Reading Guide** or **1920s NYC Map** without switching tabs.
